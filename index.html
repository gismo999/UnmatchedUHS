UHS Hospital Lockdown Policy

Authority: A hospital lockdown may only be initiated by the Police Department (PD)[cite: 415]. No member of UHS staff has the authority to order, initiate, or grant permission for a hospital lockdown under any circumstances[cite: 414].

If the Police Department initiates a lockdown, the date and time of the decision must be formally documented and immediately reported to GOLD COMMAND[cite: 416]. A hospital lockdown will only be enacted when GOLD COMMAND representatives from both UHS and the Police Department jointly determine that a lockdown is the most appropriate course of action[cite: 418].

Patient Care: In the event of a hospital lockdown, triage tents must be established without delay[cite: 421]. Members of the public requiring medical attention are to be assessed and treated within the triage tents, ensuring continuity of care while maintaining safety[cite: 422].

Common Medical Emergencies

Heart Attack (MI)

Signs: chest pain, sweating, nausea, left arm pain[cite: 674].

  • 300mg aspirin [cite: 675]
  • GTN if BP safe [cite: 676]
  • Oxygen if sats low [cite: 677]
  • Priority transport [cite: 678]

Anaphylaxis

Signs: swelling, wheeze, rash, low BP[cite: 681].

  • IM adrenaline (0.5mg) [cite: 682]
  • High-flow oxygen [cite: 683]
  • Fast transport [cite: 684]

Asthma Attack

Signs: wheeze, short of breath, speaking in short sentences[cite: 687].

  • Salbutamol (nebuliser/MDI) [cite: 688]
  • Oxygen [cite: 689]
  • Urgent transport if not improving [cite: 691]

Emergency Driving & Exemptions

Blue-light response should only be used for immediately life-threatening emergencies, urgent inter-facility transfers, major incidents, and operations requiring rapid deployment [cite: 533-537].

Exemptions Under Emergency Conditions:

  • Exceeding Speed Limits [cite: 804]
  • Travelling on the wrong side of the road [cite: 804]
  • Treating RED lights as give way [cite: 804]
  • Using audible warning systems at night [cite: 804]
  • Stopping on a motorway [cite: 804]

Speed Limits on Response

Driving at a speed exceeding 20 mph above the speed limit is not recommended[cite: 800]. Breaching this limit may result in lack of support from LAS command and could lead to the revocation of your CERAD certification[cite: 800].
